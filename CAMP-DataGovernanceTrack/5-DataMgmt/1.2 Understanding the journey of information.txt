2. From creation to insight
00:12 - 01:50
The data life cycle outlines the journey from data inception to culmination through these pivotal stages: creation and acquisition, storage, processing, use and sharing and archiving and destruction. Good management is vital at each stage of the data life cycle and this is where data life cycle management comes into play. It is a framework overseeing business data within an organization throughout its lifespan. To understand this concept, ask yourself: Where does data come from, and where does it go? Examine customer transaction data from a retail website: it is captured and stored in a database. Thereafter, it undergoes processing, followed by utilization across departments for analysis and sharing of insights. If the data remains unused, it eventually ages and can be responsibly decommissioned. Data life cycle management enables efficient data utilization, facilitating informed decision-making, and optimizing resources. Overlooking this coordination may result in data chaos, security vulnerabilities, and non-compliance with regulations. Comprehending the data life cycle and implementing robust data management processes guarantees a purposeful journey for your data, minimizing risks while maximizing intrinsic value. Let's explore the data life cycle stages.

3. Crafting data

Data creation and acquisition marks the inception of the data life cycle. During this phase, data not only comes into existence but also integrates into the organization's domain. For example, consider customer details being input into a system or the acquisition of market trends from diverse sources. Understanding this foundational phase is paramount as it sets the tone for the entire data life cycle. The quality, accuracy, and relevance of the data established here ripple through subsequent stages, influencing decisions and insights derived later on. It's not merely about gathering data. It's about ensuring its integrity and alignment with organizational objectives.

4. Data's shelter
02:41 - 03:08
The second stage in the life cycle, data storage, provides a secure haven for information. It involves choosing optimal storage solutions, from databases to the cloud, ensuring data resides in a protected environment. Efficient data storage lays the groundwork for accessibility and integrity throughout the life cycle, supporting informed decision-making.

5. From raw to refined
03:08 - 03:35

This involves using tools and techniques to refine the data, making it more meaningful. 6. Shared data, smart decisions

This phase involves collaborative utilization across departments, extracting value from insights. 7. Preservation and data goodbyes
04:03 - 04:44
As we wrap up the data life cycle, the spotlight shifts to data archiving and destruction. Archiving preserves valuable historical data for future reference and compliance, ensuring a comprehensive organizational memory. Conversely, data destruction responsibly concludes the data journey, safeguarding privacy and adhering to regulatory requirements. Together, these stages epitomize the responsible management of data, balancing the retention of valuable insights with the necessity of securely saying goodbye to data that has served its purpose.
