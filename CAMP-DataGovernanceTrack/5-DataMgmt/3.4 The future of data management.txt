5. Data mesh architecture
02:40 - 03:42
'Data mesh,' coined by Zhamak Dehghani in 2019, emerges as a transformative concept in data management. This framework organizes data into domains, treating it as a valuable product with self-service access and federated governance. For instance, in a large e-commerce platform with data mesh architecture, product data resides in a dedicated domain managed by the product team, fostering faster decision-making and team autonomy. The outcome includes enhanced product recommendations, real-time inventory insights, and a personalized shopping experience. Data mesh architecture accelerates analytics, empowering user-friendly data access and elevating intelligence, providing organizations with improved scalability, responsiveness, and innovative capabilities.
